Before you apply

The questions people ask before the call.

Is it really free? What's the catch?
Yes. ₹0, no card, no trial. We earn only if you later choose to invest through us, from product commissions that are disclosed before any transaction. The review stands on its own and you keep it either way.
Who actually does the review?
A SEBI-registered professional from the Point-1 Club team. Not a bank RM, not a sales agent, and nobody with a product target on the call.
Do I need to share passwords or bank OTPs?
Never. You share a consolidated account statement (CAS) PDF or a read-only RBI account-aggregator link. We can see your holdings. We cannot move money.
How long does it take?
Sixty seconds to apply. Forty-five minutes on the call. Your written review follows. Most people are done inside a week of applying.
Is there a minimum portfolio size?
The review works best from about ₹25 lakh invested, or ₹25,000 a month in SIPs. Smaller than that? Apply anyway. We'll tell you what to fix first.
Will you pitch me PMS?
If your portfolio qualifies for our managed strategies, we'll show you the numbers and let you decide in your own time. Nothing is bought or sold on the call.
Why the OTP?
So a real person gets a real call, and nobody can book a review in your name. It also means we don't waste your slot on a typo.
Is my data safe?
Applying takes your name and mobile number. Holdings are read-only and used only to prepare your review. We never sell data.
